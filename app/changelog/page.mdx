## What's new

Vendary is under active development — notable changes are summarized here.
For the full history, see the
[commit log](https://github.com/Maheshh-S/Vendary/commits/main).

### October 2026

- **Honest, tamper-proof checkout.** Every amount is now computed on the server
  from database prices (client cart prices are ignored), orders charge exactly
  what the cart shows (8% GST included and persisted on the order), and order
  creation is transactional with real stock tracking — items decrement on
  purchase and products flip to Out of Stock automatically at zero.
- **Razorpay webhook.** Captured payments confirm orders and processed refunds
  cancel them automatically, with signature verification and idempotent
  handling.
- **My Orders + admin order management.** Buyers see their order history with
  INR totals; admins update order statuses from the dashboard.
- **Currency selector.** The footer locale picker shows prices in your local
  currency (display only — checkout is always charged in INR).
- **Auth polish.** Timing-safe login, email verification enforcement, and
  Google sign-in.

### Known gaps on the roadmap

Password reset, saved addresses, order emails, and catalog pagination are
next — see `docs/ROADMAP.md` in the repository.
